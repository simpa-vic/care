WORKSPACE PREFERENCES

Settings & data

Manage this prototype's sample data and understand its storage limits.

Local demo data

Patient, doctor and treatment records are stored in browser localStorage. They do not sync between computers and are not protected like a production medical-record system.

Production readiness checklist

  • Backend API and relational database (e.g. MySQL or PostgreSQL)
  • Secure authentication, role-based access and session expiry
  • Audit trail for record views, edits, exports and deletions
  • Encryption in transit and at rest, backups and recovery testing
  • Consent, retention, access and breach-response policies aligned with applicable law
  • Clinical validation, duplicate detection and controlled diagnosis terminology
Prototype settings do not configure security controls for a real hospital.
